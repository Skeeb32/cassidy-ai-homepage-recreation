FAQ

Questions, answered.

Find answers about the platform, security, and getting started. Still have questions? Talk to our team.

Is my data used to train AI models?＋

No. Your data stays private. Cassidy never uses your data to train AI models—it is used solely to provide context for your automations.

How does Cassidy keep information up to date?＋

Cassidy syncs with your external data sources, so your Knowledge Base reflects the latest versions of your files. Document Verification lets your team flag potentially outdated content for review.

Can I deploy Cassidy in Slack or Microsoft Teams?＋

Yes. Cassidy can be deployed through Slack and Microsoft Teams, as well as via Chrome, Word, Excel, and Outlook extensions, so people can work where they already do.

What security certifications does Cassidy have?＋

Cassidy is SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, and CASA certified. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with granular, role-based access controls.

How is Cassidy different from other AI tools and platforms?＋

Cassidy is an automation platform built to power real business processes. Its Knowledge Base brings deep business context, its no-code tools help teams build workflows, and a dedicated solutions team supports implementation.

How are other companies using Cassidy?＋

Teams use Cassidy across sales, customer support, marketing, and operations—from answering customer questions and updating CRM records to drafting content and preparing meeting briefs.

Can Cassidy connect with my existing tools?＋

Yes. Cassidy offers integrations for knowledge sources like Google Drive, SharePoint, and OneDrive, plus workflow actions like updating Salesforce or sending Microsoft Teams messages. Custom needs can connect through webhooks and the API.
